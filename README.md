# Guru.AI 🕉️

## What is Guru.AI? 🧑‍🏫

Guru.AI is an all-encompassing tool to serve your needs as a disciple of Dharma!

Trained with over 30,000 verses of divine spiritual knowledge from Hindu theology, Guru is well-equipped to support your every step throughout the journey to explore spirituality.
Serving as an instructor, a friend, as well as a therapist, Guru will answer any questions in an understanding and warm manner, from providing insights on specific verses of Hindu holy texts to serving as a tool to supplement your emotional needs.

Guru also cites his sources, showing accurate portayals and interpretations of the holy texts that he references!

## Why Guru.AI? ❓

Guru isn't just a LLM that can read back certain scripture, he is a steward of many pillars of spiritual knowledge from multiple schools of thought. Guru serves to transform your perspective on many different aspects of the Hindu faith, from ritual to symbolism. 

"Guru", translated into english, literally means a spiritual teacher who imparts Vedic knowledge onto the disciples (Shishya), and armed with full literacy of the Vedas, Upanishads, and the renowned Bhagavad Gita, Guru is prepared to respond to ANY spiritual question. 

**DISCLAIMER** : Guru.AI is currently in development and will be integrated into the MyHindu ecosystem.

**Tech Stack** : Langchain, FAISS, GPT-4o model, Serp API, DuckduckGo API, Chainlit (UI)
